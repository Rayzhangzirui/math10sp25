# Math 10 Kaggle Competition

## Overview

This is a friendly in-class regression competition designed to help you to practice your data science skills. You can form teams of up to 2 people.

To join the competition, use the link in Canvas with your uci email.

## Dates 
* End Date: 6/2/2025 11:59 PM
* Private leaderboard revealed: 6/3/2025 8:00 AM

## Prize and Grading

This competition offers up to **5 extra credit points**:

| Achievement | Points |
|-------------|--------|
| Valid submission | 2 points |
| Match benchmark score (within 0.1 of baseline)^ | 3 points |
| Rank 4–10 on the private leaderboard | 4 points |
| Rank in the top 3 on the private leaderboard | 5 points |

^The benchmark is based on a vanilla linear regression model.

**Note:** To receive points, **top 10 teams must share their notebook** with the host for review.


## Public vs. Private Leaderboards

- **Public Leaderboard:**  
  Reflects performance on roughly 50% of the test set. Updated after each submission.

- **Private Leaderboard:**  
  Revealed only after the competition deadline. Final scores and rankings are based on remaining 50% of the test set.


## Tips and Hints

- Avoid overfitting to the public leaderboard—it doesn’t reflect final rankings.
- You can try feature engineering, polynomial regression, advanced regression models, etc. These can outperform the vanilla linear regression.
